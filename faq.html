Booking

As early as you can. Spring and autumn Saturdays fill up first, and 2027 dates are open now. Once your date and venue are locked in, send an enquiry and we will check availability. Smaller orders like bouquets usually need 3 days' notice.

Full weddings have a minimum spend of $1000. Elopements and personal-flowers-only packages start lower; see the wedding pricing.

We will reply within 3 business days to chat about your ideas. You'll then get a tailored quote. Your date is secured once the booking deposit is paid.

Mars Florals is based in Boorloo and works across the metro area and surroundings such as the Swan Valley or the South West.

Payments

A 30% deposit secures your date. The balance is due 2 weeks before the wedding. Payment details are on your invoice.

Numbers and details can usually be adjusted until 2 weeks before your date. The full cancellation and postponement terms are in the booking agreement which will be sent out with the invoice.
