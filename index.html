hi, I'm Shannon

I've always been drawn to art and nature, and floristry is where the two meet. Six years ago that love turned into a career. I have spent years working in florist studios in Boorloo, learning the craft from the ground up, before branching out into weddings and freelance work.

Now, through Mars Florals, I get to bring that same mix of art and nature to your day, with flowers that feel like you. My style is bold and a little whimsical: unexpected colour, playful shapes and flowers with personality. Working with me is a relaxed, creative collaboration. I'll listen to your ideas, add a few of my own, and shape it all into flowers that feel completely like you. No idea is too out-there.

Mars Florals works on Whadjuk Noongar boodja. We pay our respects to Elders past and present, and acknowledge that sovereignty was never ceded.
